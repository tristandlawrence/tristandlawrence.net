Megagames

Designing and running large-scale, live-action strategy games for 50 to 200 players.

In the room

Megagames are one-off, large-scale live-action games — usually 50 to 200 players split across teams, playing out a scenario in real time over several hours. I help organise and run them, currently as a board member of Melbourne Megagames, handling strategy, financial oversight and delivery for events that need the same things any large operation does: clear roles, fast decisions under uncertainty, and a design that survives contact with a room full of unpredictable people.

It's the closest thing I have to a hobby that's also a live simulation of exactly what I do for a living — building the structure that lets a large group of people make good decisions quickly, without a rulebook covering every situation they'll actually face.
