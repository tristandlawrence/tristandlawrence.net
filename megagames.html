Megagaming

Designing and running large-scale, live-action strategy games for 50 to 200 players — and helping the organisation behind them scale from a handful of volunteers into something that can reliably deliver several events a year.

In the room

Megagames are one-off, large-scale live-action games — usually 50 to 200 players split across teams, playing out a scenario in real time over several hours. I facilitate, run logistics, and lead games for Melbourne Megagames, and I've been on the board since 2025.

Scaling a volunteer organisation that runs events this size is the same problem I solve professionally: clear roles, decisions made fast under uncertainty, and a design that survives contact with a room full of unpredictable people. The work that makes it repeatable — logistics, facilitator development, and structure that doesn't depend on the same three people every time — is the part I care most about.
