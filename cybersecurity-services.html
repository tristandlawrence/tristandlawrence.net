Cybersecurity Advisory & vCISO

Fractional security leadership and focused, engagement-based advisory for organisations that need experienced judgement without a full-time hire.

Virtual CISO (vCISO)

Ongoing fractional security leadership: strategy, board and executive reporting, and risk oversight — a steady hand for a function that doesn't need, or can't yet justify, a full-time CISO.

An honest, evidence-based assessment of where your security and risk function actually stands against the framework that matters to you (ISO 27001, NIST CSF, Essential Eight, CPS 234), with a roadmap that's realistic about resourcing.

Incident Response Readiness

Reviewing or building your incident response plan, running tabletop exercises, and facilitating post-incident reviews that produce real fixes, not just a report.

Board & Executive Risk Communication

Translating cyber risk into terms your board and executive team can actually use to make decisions — board papers, briefings, and metrics that tell the truth.

Third-Party & M&A Security Due Diligence

Vendor risk reviews, and security due diligence for acquisitions — on either side of the table.

Interim Security Leadership

Covering a security or technology leadership gap during a search, restructure, or transition.
