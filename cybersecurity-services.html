Problems I help solve

Most organisations don't call me because they've had a breach. They call because something isn't adding up — the reporting says one thing and the room knows another, or security has become a bottleneck nobody wants to own.

  • “The board keeps asking whether we're secure, and nobody can answer honestly.”
  • “Everything is green in the report and we all quietly know it isn't.”
  • “We've grown past the point where security can be someone's side-of-desk job.”
  • “We only find out about risk once it's already a problem.”
  • “If we had a serious incident tomorrow, we'd be making it up on the day.”
  • “We're losing deals because we can't credibly answer customer security questions.”
  • “Our security leader just left and we don't know what we've inherited.”

Where to start

Where are you stuck?

  • “We have no security leadership — or it just walked out the door.”

    → Virtual CISO  or  Interim Security Leadership

  • “We don't actually know how exposed we are.”

    → Security & Risk Maturity Assessment

  • “We'd be improvising on the day of an incident.”

    → Incident Response Readiness

  • “The board nods along, then nothing changes.”

    → Board & Executive Risk Communication

  • “We're buying, selling, or trusting someone else with our data.”

    → Third-Party & M&A Security Due Diligence

Not sure which line you're on? That's a normal place to start — the first conversation is usually about working out which problem you actually have.

Services

Virtual CISO (vCISO)

Ongoing fractional security leadership: strategy, board and executive reporting, and risk oversight — a steady hand for a function that doesn't need, or can't yet justify, a full-time CISO.

Security & Risk Maturity Assessment

An honest, evidence-based assessment of where your security and risk function actually stands against the framework that matters to you (ISO 27001, NIST CSF, Essential Eight, CPS 234), with a roadmap that's realistic about resourcing.

Incident Response Readiness

Reviewing or building your incident response plan, running tabletop exercises, and facilitating post-incident reviews that produce real fixes, not just a report.

Board & Executive Risk Communication

Translating cyber risk into terms your board and executive team can actually use to make decisions — board papers, briefings, and metrics that tell the truth.

Third-Party & M&A Security Due Diligence

Vendor risk reviews, and security due diligence for acquisitions — on either side of the table.

Interim Security Leadership

Covering a security or technology leadership gap during a search, restructure, or transition.

This list is a starting point rather than a fixed menu — every engagement starts with a conversation about what you actually need.
