Where to start

Structure and culture problems rarely announce themselves, but there are signs your structure is slowing you down.

Which one sounds like you?

  • Decisions stall, or get made at the wrong level.

    • Because leadership can't make the decision.

      → Start with Leadership Coaching, or a supporting planning process.

    • Because too many people are involved in making it.

      → Start with Organisational Design.

  • Problems don't get raised.

    • They surface at the last minute.

      • Because people don't want to be accountable for risks.

        → Start with a Risk Culture Uplift.

      • Because there's no mechanism for raising them.

        → Start with Governance Design.

These problems tend to travel together. Most engagements start with one and touch two or three.

Services

Team & Organisational Design

Structuring a security, risk or technology function so accountability, skills and workload line up — role design, skills frameworks, hiring plans, and succession.

Risk Culture Uplift

Building the habits, incentives and psychological safety that get people to report problems early instead of hiding them — the single best predictor of whether a risk function actually works.

Governance Design

Designing committees, charters, decision rights and risk delegation frameworks that give people the authority to make decisions at the right level.

Leadership Coaching

Coaching technology, risk and security leaders — and the peers and managers around them — on planning, prioritisation, and stakeholder management.

Operating Model Design

Planning cadences, metrics and agile ceremonies that keep a technology or risk team accountable without drowning it in process.

This list is a starting point rather than a fixed menu — every engagement starts with a conversation about what you actually need.
