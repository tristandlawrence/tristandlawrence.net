About

I've spent over 15 years in cybersecurity and more than a decade leading teams — three times inheriting a small, under-resourced function and building it into a real one. I'm a people-first capability builder: clarity lets people perform, focuses energy, and helps them anticipate risk before it becomes a crisis.

My focus now is vCISO advisory, organisational design, and risk culture — helping organisations get honest, working risk management instead of risk theatre. I've led cyber security, IT and platform functions at Monash Health, HotDoc and MessageMedia, and consulted into a neobank, MYOB and Telstra before that.

Outside of work I run tabletop role-playing games, help organise large-scale live-action megagames, and lead a community music theatre organisation.
