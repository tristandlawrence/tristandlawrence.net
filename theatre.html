Community Music Theatre

Performing, producing and leading a community theatre organisation.

On stage

On stage in an early production — the same instinct that runs a cyber team runs a cast: give people clarity, and let them perform.

I've been involved in community music theatre for close to two decades, on stage and behind the scenes. Committee roles, production management, and now steering a community organisation through the same pressures every volunteer-run group faces: declining volunteer numbers, ageing systems, and the constant work of keeping people engaged without burning them out.

It's a smaller stage than a hospital network or a listed company, but the underlying problem is identical to the one I solve professionally: getting a group of people — most of them volunteers, all of them busy — pointed in the same direction, with enough structure that things don't fall over, and enough trust that they don't have to be told twice.
